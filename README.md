WPSD-Tool is a simple helper to identify, safe and restore changes in your dmrgateway configuration files, that are altered every time you change something in your settings, even if it´s just a display parameter! those overwrites are hard coded by the WPSD team (arguably for safety reasons) and can´t be changed by yourself!
You can simply download the manual, run it on any system an follow the suggestions to go from there!

Please be aware, this is an experimental beta version! You´re free to do anything at your own risc!
